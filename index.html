Key Projects

Port Said Coastal Monitoring & Flood Prediction Digital Twin

Developed an advanced geospatial project dedicated to monitoring coastline retreat and simulating potential flash flood risks at any given time across the Port Said Governorate. This comprehensive analysis is dynamic and fully integrated into an interactive, next-generation dashboard deployed on Google Colab, utilizing a completely unique and innovative design philosophy built entirely from scratch. The initiative directly supports the UN Sustainable Development Goals, strongly aligning with SDG 6 (Clean Water), SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities), SDG 13 (Climate Action), and SDG 14 (Life Below Water) to foster data-driven climate resilience for UNDP frameworks.

Key Methodologies & Technologies:

  • Model Validation: Utilized the Kappa coefficient to verify classification accuracy, achieving a highly reliable result of 0.83.
  • Sabkha Classification: Implemented a Random Forest machine learning algorithm optimized with customized Short-Wave Infrared (SWIR) bands to isolate and map coastal salt flats (Sabkhas).
  • Cloud & AI Integration: Integrated GCP (Google Cloud Platform) for seamless imagery ingestion, combined with SAM 2 (Segment Anything Model 2) for advanced object segmentation.
  • Sensor Comparison Slider: Developed an interactive comparison slider to perform precise side-by-side temporal analysis between Sentinel-1 SAR (Backscatter) and Sentinel-2 optical data.
