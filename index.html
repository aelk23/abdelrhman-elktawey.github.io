Key Projects

Port Said Coastal Monitoring & Flood Prediction Digital Twin

Developed an advanced geospatial project dedicated to monitoring coastline retreat and simulating potential flash flood risks at any given time across the Port Said Governorate. This comprehensive analysis is dynamic and fully integrated into an interactive, next-generation dashboard deployed on Google Colab, utilizing a completely unique and innovative design philosophy built entirely from scratch. The initiative directly supports the UN Sustainable Development Goals, strongly aligning with SDG 6 (Clean Water), SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities), SDG 13 (Climate Action), and SDG 14 (Life Below Water) to foster data-driven climate resilience for UNDP frameworks. I used under 8000 training points the Random Foreststrong> algorithm. I separated the sabkhas using computer vision, Deep learning, and Machine learning because, with their geophysical properties, their reflected radiation shares some of the wavebands reflected from the sea surfaces and the Suez Canal in the area. As previously mentioned, spectral indicators such as AWEL, Random forest NIR and SWIR Traning have been used in order to isolate any concrete surface, and here I mean the sea pavement and sand on the beaches and ports in eastern Port Said, because their spectral bands overlap with some of the near infrared bands of the sea and the Suez Canal, so I isolated their spectral bands to reduce and eliminate the interference. I used a programming style in writing codes that relied on managing server resources and not consuming the T4 graphics card as violently as possible in Google Colab.

Key Methodologies & Technologies:

  • Model Validation: Utilized the Kappa coefficient to verify classification accuracy, achieving a highly reliable result of 0.83.
  • Sabkha Classification: Implemented a Random Forest machine learning algorithm optimized with customized Short-Wave Infrared (SWIR) bands to isolate and map coastal salt flats (Sabkhas).
  • Cloud & AI Integration: Integrated GCP (Google Cloud Platform) for seamless imagery ingestion, combined with SAM 2 (Segment Anything Model 2) for advanced object segmentation.
  • Sensor Comparison Slider: Developed an interactive comparison slider to perform precise side-by-side temporal analysis between Sentinel-1 SAR (Backscatter) and Sentinel-2 optical data.
